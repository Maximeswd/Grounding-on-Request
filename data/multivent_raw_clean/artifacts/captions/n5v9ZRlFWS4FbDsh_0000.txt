Witness the breathtaking evolution of the Harbin Ice and Snow Festival, showcasing masterpieces from its inception in 1963 to its modern-day grandeur. The festival features intricate ice sculptures of mythical dragons, armored warriors, and Disney’s Elsa, alongside elaborate ice castles and architectural marvels. Visitors explore vast ice-themed landscapes, illuminated at night with vibrant lights and fireworks, creating a magical winter wonderland that attracts tourists from around the world.
