A large crowd of protesters marches down a city street under bright sunlight, waving colorful flags—including purple, green, red, and yellow banners with Korean text—and holding signs demanding the abolition of the National Pension Fund (내란공범,국립해체). The atmosphere is charged with activism as demonstrators move forward in solidarity.
