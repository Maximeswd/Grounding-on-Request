A crowd of protesters gathers outdoors on a sunny day, holding signs and banners in Korean. One prominent white banner reads "내란수괴 윤석열 체포! 구속!" (Arrest and detain the leader of the coup, Yoon Suk-yeol!) alongside a logo for "한국노총" (Korean Confederation of Trade Unions). Another sign points toward "City Hall" and "Namseon 1-gil Tunnel." The demonstrators, bundled in winter coats, hold flags including a white one with the same union logo. A large white tent is set up behind them, with additional banners visible.
