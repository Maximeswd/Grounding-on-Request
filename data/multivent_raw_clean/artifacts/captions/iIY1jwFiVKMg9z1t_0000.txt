A composite sequence documenting the launch of the HLA-2 (Hongliang-2) orbital rocket from a sea-based platform. The imagery progresses from ground-level preparations on the launch barge, where technicians are seen near the white rocket stage marked with "ORIENT SPACE" and "HLA" logos, to the dramatic ignition and liftoff. Subsequent frames capture the vehicle's ascent through thick smoke and clouds, culminating in a high-altitude shot of the rocket piercing the sky, showcasing the power of its solid-fuel boosters and central core.
