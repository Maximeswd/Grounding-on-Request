A somber memorial scene features a large white teddy bear, smaller plush toys including a pink pig and a tiger, and a framed black-and-white photograph of an airplane with the registration RA-47315. The frame bears the Russian word "СКОРБИМ" (We Mourn) and the date "24.07.25 | АМУРСКАЯ ОБЛАСТЬ" (Amur Region). The tribute is lit by a melting pillar candle, a tea light, and a bouquet of red roses, all arranged on a granite surface at night.
