A large crowd participates in the "Immortal Regiment" parade, marching down a city street. Participants of all ages, including children, teenagers, and elderly individuals, carry portraits of their relatives who served in the Soviet Armed Forces. A prominent orange banner reads "БЕССМЕРТНЫЙ ПОЛК - СЫКТЫВКА" (Immortal Regiment - Syktyvkar). People are dressed in casual clothing, with some wearing military-style caps or holding flags, creating a solemn yet unified atmosphere of remembrance.
