A time-lapse sequence captures the dynamic dance of the aurora borealis over a cityscape at night. The sky transitions from a deep, star-filled indigo to vibrant, swirling ribbons of green and teal light, characteristic of the northern lights. Below, the urban skyline remains static, featuring illuminated apartment blocks with warm yellow lights from windows, creating a striking contrast with the cool, ethereal glow above. Faint trails of aircraft contrails occasionally streak across the celestial display, adding a subtle layer of human activity to the natural phenomenon.
