Heavy rainfall floods dozens of tents in Western Idlib's Kherbet, as seen in aerial views of submerged makeshift shelters and muddy streets. Rescue teams from The White Helmets navigate the inundated areas in rain gear, responding to the disaster. Locals, including children, wade through the floodwaters amidst damaged structures and debris, while powerful, turbulent currents surge through the settlement, illustrating the severe impact of the storm on the community.
