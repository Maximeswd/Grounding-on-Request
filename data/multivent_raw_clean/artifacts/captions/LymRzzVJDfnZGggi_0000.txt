Devastating floods in western Iran have caused catastrophic infrastructure failure and widespread inundation. The historic Kashkan Bridge, a vital link between two major cities, has been severely damaged, with its arches partially submerged in raging, muddy river currents. In Khorram A'bad, a modern highway bridge has suffered a dramatic collapse, with a large section of the roadway sheared off and plunging into the swollen river below. Further north in Ilam province, the Majin Bridge has also collapsed, leaving only a skeletal framework amidst the floodwaters.
