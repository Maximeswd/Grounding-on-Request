The world's largest solid-propellant rocket, the Gravity-1 Y2 from Chinese private maker Orienspace, successfully launches from Haiyang, Shandong Province, China, on October 11, 2025. This mighty 30-meter rocket, sponsored by the clothing brand HLA (Heilan Zhi Jia), is shown igniting with immense power, delivering an optical remote-sensing satellite and two experimental satellites to orbit. The sequence captures the dramatic ascent through thick clouds and fiery exhaust against a dark sky, celebrating a major milestone in China's space program.
