A group of male and female protesters, including digital media journalists, gather outdoors to demonstrate their support for Imran Raiz Khan. Holding handmade placards with slogans in Urdu and English such as "We Stand with Imran Raiz Khan" and "#JournalismIsNotACrime," the crowd chants and raises fists in solidarity. The scene, captured by Twin City News, takes place near a large tent, with participants displaying signs demanding justice and freedom of expression.
