This series of news clips from YTN documents the severe weather conditions in Busan, South Korea, prior to the landfall of Typhoon Hinnamnor on September 5th. The footage captures the immense power of the storm from multiple locations, including Songdo Seawater Bathhouse, Minrak Seaside Park, and Gwangalli Beach. Viewers witness towering, churning waves crashing violently against seawalls and breakwaters, both during the day under overcast skies and at night illuminated by streetlights and purple floodlights. The scenes depict a chaotic environment with massive water surges threatening coastal infrastructure, highlighting the dangerous situation
