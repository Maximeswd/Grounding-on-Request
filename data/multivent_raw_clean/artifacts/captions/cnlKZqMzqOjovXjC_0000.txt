This video sequence documents the final approach and crash of JAS Air flight 7C2216. The first several frames are screenshots from Flightradar24, tracking the aircraft's path from Vietnam, over the East China Sea, and past Taiwan, before it descends towards Muan Airport in South Korea. The final frames show the aftermath, with a massive plume of black smoke billowing from the impact site near the airport, followed by a close-up shot of thick white smoke and emergency responders in protective gear on the ground.
