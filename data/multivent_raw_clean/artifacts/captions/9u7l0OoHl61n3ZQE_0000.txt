A massive fire engulfs a multi-story commercial building in Cheonan, Republic of Korea, sending thick plumes of black smoke billowing into the sky. Emergency responders, including firefighters in protective gear and operating large ladder trucks, work to extinguish the blaze, directing powerful streams of water onto the burning structure. The building's facade is severely damaged, with charred remains, exposed beams, and debris visible amidst the intense flames and smoke. The scene captures the scale of the disaster and the coordinated efforts of the fire department at the intersection of a city street.
