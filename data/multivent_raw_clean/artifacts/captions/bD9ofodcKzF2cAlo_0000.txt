This series of satellite and thermal imagery captures the nocturnal volcanic activity of Mount Etna in Sicily, Italy. The main feed, along with various thermal channels (labeled A, C, L, M, N, O, V), documents intermittent explosive eruptions. Bright incandescent lava fountains and glowing embers are seen bursting from the summit crater, illuminating the surrounding darkness. The thermal views, particularly in the infrared spectrum, reveal the intense heat of the active lava flows and the expansive, glowing ash plumes billowing into the atmosphere. The sequence highlights the dynamic and powerful nature of this active stratovolcano
