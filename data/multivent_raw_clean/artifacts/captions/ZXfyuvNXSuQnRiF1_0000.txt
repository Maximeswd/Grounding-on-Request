On December 24, 2019, a massive explosion and fire engulfed the Posco Gwangyang Steelworks in Gwangyang-si, Jeollanam-do, South Korea. Thick black smoke billows into the sky, partially obscuring the nearby Lee Sun-shin Bridge. The incident involved a secondary explosion within the factory's power generation equipment, leading to the bridge's closure due to hazardous fumes. Five factory employees were injured and hospitalized following the blast.
