The 2025 Seoul Mobility Show showcases cutting-edge automotive and robotics technology, featuring Boston Dynamics' industrial inspection robot SPOT at a Clobot booth, customizable corporate card keys from IONIQ, and futuristic concept cars with immersive interiors. Visitors explore various exhibits, including a hydrogen fuel cell NEXO SUV under a simulated rain display, a sleek gray SUV with an open hood and vibrant yellow interior, and a striking orange Mercedes-AMG G-Class, all set against the backdrop of a bustling, modern exhibition hall filled with attendees.
