Dashcam footage captures a massive explosion and fireball erupting from a power generation facility under test near the POSCO Pohang Steelworks in Gwangyang, South Jeolla Province. The incident, which occurred on the 24th, sent a thick plume of black smoke and a fiery trail high into the sky, visible from a nearby bridge. The blast resulted in one worker being critically injured and four others sustaining minor injuries. Firefighting efforts were successful in fully suppressing the blaze approximately 20 minutes after it began.
