A series of video frames captures a protest outside the "Women's Prison" (اتحادیه خوزستان), featuring groups of women and men holding signs in Persian. The demonstrators, some in traditional black chadors and others in casual attire, hold placards with slogans like "Prisoners must be freed" (کارگران زندانی آزاد باید گردد) and "Workplace is a prison" (جای کار گر در کارخانه هست جای دزد در زندان). The scenes alternate between the prison entrance and other locations, highlighting the ongoing demonstration for the release of incarcerated workers.
