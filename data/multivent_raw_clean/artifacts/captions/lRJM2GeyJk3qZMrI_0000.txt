Visitors explore the Harbin Ice & Snow Festival in China, walking across massive, sculpted ice bridges and pathways. The scene features towering ice castles and colorful, illuminated structures under a twilight sky. People in winter clothing, including puffy jackets and fur hats, navigate the slippery, translucent ice surfaces, with some sliding down gentle inclines. The festive atmosphere is filled with crowds enjoying the unique, frozen architecture and winter entertainment.
