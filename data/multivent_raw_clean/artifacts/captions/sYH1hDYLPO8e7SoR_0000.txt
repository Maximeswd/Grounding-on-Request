The aftermath of deadly floods in Zhengzhou, China, following the heaviest rainfall in 60 years. The images depict a chaotic scene with numerous vehicles submerged or damaged, many cars overturned, and large crowds of people wading through flooded streets and walking along elevated walkways. Some individuals are using umbrellas and raincoats. The situation is described as 'extremely severe' by President Xi Jinping, and scientists attribute the worsening flooding to climate change and increasingly extreme weather patterns.
