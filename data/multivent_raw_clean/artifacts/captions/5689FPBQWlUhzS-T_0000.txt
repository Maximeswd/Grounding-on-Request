A large, energetic crowd of protesters marches down a city street, waving signs and rainbow flags in a passionate demonstration. Participants of all ages, including a young boy chanting and older men raising their fists, hold up placards with Korean slogans such as "새봄에는 새나라로" (To a new country in the new spring) and "윤석열 파면" (Yoon Suk-yeol impeached). The atmosphere is charged with collective action, as people chant slogans, wave signs, and capture the moment on their phones, united in their cause.
