Simon Loasby, Senior Vice President of the Hyundai Design Center, presents the new IONIQ 6 N-Line sedan on a modern stage. The sleek, aerodynamic electric vehicle is showcased in both a vibrant red and a sophisticated light blue color, with its distinctive futuristic design highlighted by sharp lines and advanced lighting. The presentation emphasizes the car's innovative aesthetics and performance-oriented features, drawing attention from an audience and photographers.
