This video documents the aftermath of a historic snowstorm in Russia's Far East, which brought the region's heaviest snowfall in nearly 60 years. The footage captures the immense scale of the accumulation, showing streets and sidewalks buried under deep drifts that reach waist-high on pedestrians. Cars are seen driving cautiously through slushy roads or completely buried, with only their roofs visible. The urban landscape is transformed, with storefronts like "Dustin Barbershop" and "Wildberries" partially obscured by massive mounds of snow piled against buildings.
