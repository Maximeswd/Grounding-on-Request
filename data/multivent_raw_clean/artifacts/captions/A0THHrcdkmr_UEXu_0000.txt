In a groundbreaking event at the 2025 Beijing Yizhuang Half Marathon, a humanoid robot, identified as "Tiangong," competes alongside human runners, becoming the first robot to complete the race. The video captures the robot sprinting down the track, passing enthusiastic spectators and event staff clad in green vests, before crossing the finish line under the archway. A large digital clock displays the race time, highlighting the robot's impressive speed and performance in this world's inaugural robot half-marathon.
