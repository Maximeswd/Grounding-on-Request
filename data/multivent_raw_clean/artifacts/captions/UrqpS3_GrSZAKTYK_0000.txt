A large crowd of people, including children in military-style uniforms and adults in civilian clothes, march down a paved road carrying red flags and portraits. A prominent red banner with white Cyrillic text, reading "полки човидон 10 бессмертный полк душе таджик" (Chovidon 10 regiments, Immortal Regiment, soul of Tajikistan), is held aloft by the participants. The procession moves forward along the tree-lined street under an overcast sky.
