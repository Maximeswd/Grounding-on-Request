A spectacular nighttime light show featuring a massive, multi-level LED screen installation displaying vibrant, futuristic cityscapes and abstract digital art in blues, purples, and neon pinks. Powerful laser beams shoot into the dark sky, creating dynamic patterns above the crowd. Large banners at the base of the structure advertise the "2024 Guangzhou International Light Festival" (GZLIGHT) and the "Vibrant Bay Area, New Color Guangzhou" campaign, with enthusiastic spectators gathered in the foreground to watch the dazzling visual spectacle.
